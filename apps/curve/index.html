pedersen commitments

two (value, blinding) pairs → three commitments. the protocol's additive-homomorphism contract: C(a,r_a) + C(b,r_b) ≡ C(a+b, r_a+r_b). the third point — labelled C_a+b — is computed via the right-hand side; the left-hand side would equal it in the group (point addition not shown here, but verified at the byte level by the rust + wasm test suites).

schnorr signatures

sign a field-element message with a randomized seed. the signature is (R, s); the panel plots PK and R and verifies s·G == R + c·PK in-page. the tamper button adds 1 to s mod l to demo the rejection path — when tampered, the verification line turns red and the connecting stroke follows.
