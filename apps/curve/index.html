What you are looking at

Each dot is k·G for some k. The coordinates are 254-bit field elements, so plotted directly they look like noise in a square — that randomness is the discrete-log hardness. The connecting path shows scalar multiplication as a walk that eventually cycles through the whole prime-order subgroup.
